Counter Experiment
~en~0
An experiment designed to retest a hypothesis'
scientific reliability, and to see if any
counter-evidence can be found against it. The term is
misleading; a counter experiment is not meant to spurn
its hypothesis. If a hypothesis endures a counter
experiment (no counter-evidence found), the
hypothesis gains reliability. If a hypothesis can't
endure a counter experiment (counter-evidence
found), the hypothesis loses reliability. No further
connotation is attached to the term.
